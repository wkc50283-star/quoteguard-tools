Risk Buffer

Risk buffer is not wasted money.

A risk buffer is money you set aside for problems that may happen during or after the job. If rework, warranty, delays, mistakes, or unexpected events do not happen, the unused buffer may become extra profit.

  • Rework or mistake buffer
  • Material waste buffer
  • Warranty or return visit buffer
  • Unexpected event buffer
  • Site condition and protection cost

Cash Flow

Profit does not always mean safe cash flow.

A job can show a profit on paper and still create cash flow pressure. If you need to pay for materials, labor, tools, or subcontractors before the customer pays you, the job may require more cash than expected.

A profitable job can still hurt your business if payment comes too late.

About

What this tool does and does not do.

This tool was built to help small service businesses think beyond materials and labor. It encourages users to include real job costs, risk buffers, tool costs, rework, warranty, payment delays, and cash flow pressure before accepting a job.

This tool does not provide:

  • Legal, tax, insurance, or compliance advice
  • Safety regulation or labor regulation judgment
  • Market price or fair price judgment
  • Formal quote, invoice, contract, or bid document generation

All numbers are entered by the user. The result is only an estimate for business planning.
