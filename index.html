FAQ

Is this a construction cost calculator?

No. This calculator does not estimate full construction costs. It only shows how material cost increases and discounts may affect the profit of a job quote.

Does this tool predict material prices?

No. You enter your own material cost increase percentage. The tool does not predict steel, copper, lumber, fuel, shipping, currency, tariff, or market price changes.

Who can use this calculator?

Small contractors, handymen, renovation workers, material resellers, cleaning businesses, and other small service businesses that quote jobs before all costs are final.

What is a safe profit margin?

There is no universal safe margin. It depends on your industry, local market, overhead, risk, and business goals. Use the target margin field as your own benchmark.

Why does a discount reduce profit so much?

A discount reduces your revenue, not your costs. If material and labor costs stay the same, even a small discount can reduce your profit margin quickly.

What happens if my profit after discount is negative?

If your profit after discount is negative, the quote may result in a loss based on the numbers you entered. You may need to raise the quote, reduce costs, reduce the discount, or review whether the job is worth accepting.

Can I use this calculator for cleaning or service jobs?

Yes. This calculator can also be used for cleaning, detailing, repair, maintenance, delivery, and other service jobs as long as you enter your own quote, labor, material, and other costs.
