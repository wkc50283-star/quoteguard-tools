FAQ

Is this a construction cost calculator?

No. This is not a full construction cost calculator. It does not estimate project scope, labor hours, permits, taxes, or local market rates. It only checks how your own cost inputs may affect your job profit.

Does this calculator predict material prices?

No. This calculator does not predict future material prices. You enter your own expected material cost increase percentage to test a possible scenario.

Who can use this calculator?

Small contractors, handymen, cleaning businesses, repair workers, maintenance providers, material resellers, and small service businesses can use this tool to review simple job quote scenarios.

What is a safe profit margin?

A safe profit margin depends on your business, industry, risk, overhead, and local market. This calculator does not recommend a standard margin. You enter your own target margin.

Why does a customer discount affect profit so much?

A discount reduces your quoted price, but your costs usually stay the same. This means the discount may reduce your profit faster than expected.

What happens if my profit after discount is negative?

If your profit after discount is negative, the quote may result in a loss based on the numbers you entered. You may need to raise the quote, reduce costs, reduce the discount, or review whether the job is worth accepting.

Can I use this calculator for cleaning or service jobs?

Yes. This calculator can also be used for cleaning, detailing, repair, maintenance, delivery, and other service jobs as long as you enter your own quote, labor, material, and other costs.
