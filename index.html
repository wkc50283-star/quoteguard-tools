Why Risk Buffers Matter

A job can look profitable on paper but still lose money if rework, waste, warranty visits, site conditions, or protection costs are not included.

A risk buffer is not wasted money. If the risk does not happen, the reserved amount may become extra profit. If the risk does happen, the buffer may help prevent the job from turning into a loss.

Cash Flow Warning

A job can show profit but still create cash pressure if you must pay for materials, labor, or subcontractors before the customer pays you.

This calculator estimates the maximum cash needed and cash gap based on the upfront payments and available cash you enter.
