What this calculator does

This rental vacancy profit impact calculator helps landlords, small property managers, and small rental owners estimate whether a rental may still make money after vacancy, repairs, management fees, and other costs.

It is designed for quick scenario testing. Instead of only calculating a vacancy rate, it focuses on the question that matters before you trust the numbers: will the rental still make money after costs?

Why vacancy matters

Vacancy can quickly change a rental from profitable to risky. Fixed costs such as mortgage payments, insurance, taxes, HOA fees, and maintenance may continue even when the unit is empty.

This is why a rental should not be judged only by monthly rent. A safer estimate includes vacancy loss, repairs, management fees, and other costs before looking at net profit.

Costs to include before you trust the numbers

  • Mortgage or monthly financing costs
  • Property taxes and insurance
  • HOA or building fees
  • Property management fees
  • Repairs and maintenance
  • Cleaning, advertising, software, or inspection costs
  • Expected vacancy days per year

What the risk status means

Safe

The rental appears to stay above your target profit margin under the current assumptions. This does not guarantee profit, but the numbers have more room.

Tight

The rental is still profitable, but the margin is below your target. A small increase in vacancy, repairs, or fixed costs could make the deal risky.

Loss

The rental may lose money under the current assumptions. Review rent, vacancy days, repair costs, management fees, or fixed costs before relying on the deal.

FAQ

What is vacancy loss in a rental property?

Vacancy loss is the rental income you lose when a rental property is empty and not collecting rent.

How do I calculate vacancy loss?

A simple method is to divide annual rent by 365 to estimate daily rent, then multiply that number by the expected vacancy days per year.

Does this calculator include property management fees?

Yes. You can enter a property management fee percentage. The calculator estimates the fee based on effective rental income after vacancy.

What does break-even vacancy days mean?

Break-even vacancy days is a simplified estimate of how many vacancy days the rental can absorb before the profit reaches zero under the numbers you entered.

Can I use this for short-term rentals?

You can use it for simple income and cost scenarios, but this calculator does not check local short-term rental laws, licensing rules, tax requirements, or platform rules.

No. This calculator is for simple scenario estimates only. It is not financial, legal, tax, real estate, or investment advice.
