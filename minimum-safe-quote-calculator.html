How This Calculator Works

This calculator adds your material cost, labor cost, and other costs to estimate your total job cost. It then calculates the lowest quote needed to protect the target profit margin you enter.

For example, covering costs is not the same as making a profit. If your total cost is too close to your quote, a small mistake, delay, discount, or cost increase may remove most of your profit.

FAQ

What is a minimum safe quote?

A minimum safe quote is the lowest price you may need to charge to cover your costs and protect your target profit margin based on the numbers you enter.

Is this a contractor pricing calculator?

It can be used by contractors, but it does not estimate full construction costs or provide industry pricing standards. It only calculates a simple minimum quote based on your own costs and target margin.

Can I use this for cleaning or service jobs?

Yes. You can use this calculator for cleaning, detailing, repair, maintenance, delivery, and other service jobs as long as you enter your own costs.

Why is break-even quote different from minimum safe quote?

A break-even quote only covers your costs. A minimum safe quote also includes room for your target profit margin.

What happens if I enter a customer discount?

The calculator estimates the quote you may need before discount so that the final discounted price can still protect your target margin.
